## About Me

I'm a third-year Computer Science Engineering student at **HKBK College of Engineering (VTU, 2023–2027)**, currently interning as an **Android Development Intern at Prodigy InfoTech** — building real-world mobile applications and strengthening hands-on Android development skills.

I'm passionate about building **deployable, impactful software**. My recent work spans AI agent development, Android apps, open-source contributions (including a merged PR to [scribe-org/Scribe-Data](https://github.com/scribe-org/Scribe-Data)), and a final year AI mentorship platform — **EduMentor**. I'm also an active member of my college **IEEE Student Chapter**.

Open to collaborations, open-source projects, and opportunities in Android, AI/ML, or full-stack development.
